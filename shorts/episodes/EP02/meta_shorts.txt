TITLE (shorts):
₩26,000 for ALL of this?! Free refills + Korea's rice tea trick 🍚☕ #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Korean sotbap (pot rice) restaurant in Seoul: squid & pork stir-fry ₩14,000, grilled mackerel ₩12,000. Every side dish is free, and the japchae and tteokbokki are unlimited refills from the self-serve bar.
Then the trick nobody tells tourists: scoop the rice out, pour hot water into the pot, lid on, 3 minutes → nurungji (scorched rice) tea. Same pot, twice.
Would you drink your rice pot? 👇
