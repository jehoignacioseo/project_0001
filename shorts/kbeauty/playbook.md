# K-Beauty Shorts Playbook (what the AI editor optimizes for)

Audience: non-Korean viewers (US/EU/SEA, 18-34, mostly women, K-beauty curious or already buying).
Goal per video: watch-through (>70% completion), saves, shares, comments ("where do I buy this?"), follows.
Languages: English VO + English captions by default. Korean product names shown in romanized form plus Hangul once.

## Platform signals (2026)
- TikTok: the first 3 seconds decide distribution. Videos that lose the test pool early stop expanding. Aim for >5s qualified views
  and a 70%+ completion rate; pattern-interrupt (cut, zoom, caption change) every 2-4s. 21-45s is the sweet spot for one complete idea.
- Instagram Reels: watch time, replays, shares and DM sends rank highest. Original footage only (reposted content is removed from
  recommendations since April 2026). Trial Reels let you test a hook on non-followers first: always produce 2-3 alternative hooks.
- YouTube Shorts: the July 2026 "inauthentic content" policy demonetizes generic template content and AI personas on health topics.
  Original phone footage + an editorial point of view + a real on-screen opinion keeps us safe. Toggle the "altered or synthetic content"
  disclosure for AI voice. From October 2026 the recommender further prioritizes original Shorts.
- All three: text-on-screen captions are mandatory (most views are muted), vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, loud enough (-14 LUFS).

## Formats that are working for K-beauty right now (pick ONE per video)
1. "What Koreans actually buy" / retail proof: empty shelf, sold-out tag, ranking sticker (올리브영 1위) as credibility. Hook on the proof.
2. Price shock: same product Korea vs US/EU price, shown with a cash SFX and a 2-line price card. Works best with a visible price tag.
3. Ingredient-led explainer: one hero ingredient (PDRN, heartleaf, mugwort, rice, snail, centella, spicule), what it does in plain English,
   who it is for, who should skip it. Anti-hype tone ("not for everyone") raises trust and comments.
4. Texture / application ASMR: close-up of pad, cushion, balm texture with a 1-line fact per shot. Keep natural sound, light music.
5. Shelf tour countdown: "3 things under $15 at Olive Young" with a progress indicator (#1/3). Fast cuts, each item gets price + why.
6. New arrival alert: "this launched this week in Korea" with the exact date and one reason it will trend. Urgency drives shares.
7. Before/after or swatch comparison: split or sequential with a timer caption. Only when the footage actually shows it.
8. Mistake / myth: "you're using toner pads wrong" style correction. Open with the mistake, pay off with the fix.

## Hook rules (first 3 seconds)
- The hook is said by the VO AND shown as a 2-line title band at the same moment, large, high contrast, in the top-safe zone.
- Use one of: a number ("3 products…"), a contradiction ("stop buying…"), a secret ("Koreans don't use this for…"), a proof
  ("sold out 3 times…"), a price ("$9 here, $34 in the US"), or a direct call-out ("if you have oily skin, watch this").
- Never start with "Hi", a logo, a store exterior, or slow walking footage. Start on the product or the proof.
- Always generate 3 alternative hooks (for Trial Reels / A-B tests) in addition to the chosen one.

## Structure (25-45s)
- 0-3s hook (VO + title band), 3-8s context (where, what, price), 8-30s value (3-5 facts with cuts every 2-4s), 30-38s verdict
  (who it is for / skip if), 38-45s CTA (one specific ask: "comment your skin type", "save this for your Seoul trip"). End card ≤2.5s.
- One idea per video. Three facts beat ten. Each fact must be concrete (number, ingredient, price, date, place).

## Pacing and variety (immersion)
- A visual change every 2-3.5s: cut, push-in, insert or card. Every footage cut gets a slow push-in (zoom to +0.08..0.15); hand and
  application shots run at 1.1-1.25x. Reveals (price, verdict) get a punch (emphasis word + SFX + riser before a price).
- Inserts every 2nd-3rd cut: official or retailer pack shot (labelled with the source), reviewer photo, or a review-quote slide with the
  star rating. 1.8-3.0s each, narration keeps flowing. Never two inserts back to back; never an insert as the hook.
- Fixed black title bar on top (series name + episode/product) so the viewer always knows what they are watching; the hook title band
  shows under it for the first 2.5s.
- Music: upbeat, clean beat at -17 dB, ducked (not muted) under the voice so energy stays between lines; whoosh on scene changes,
  click on inserts, cash + riser on the price reveal, pop on cards. Natural footage sound stays audible on texture shots.

## Captions
- Karaoke-style word highlight synced to the VO (2-4 words per line, current word in yellow). Centered, lower-middle, inside safe zones.
- Keep captions ≤ 28 characters per line. Emphasis words in caps. Emojis only in on-screen cards, not in karaoke lines.
- Product names: romanized + brand, with Hangul once in the product card (e.g., "Anua Heartleaf 77% Toner · 어성초 77").

## Voice
- Natural, confident, slightly fast (1.05-1.12x), conversational "friend who lives in Seoul" tone. Short sentences. No hype adjectives
  stacking ("amazing incredible"). One opinion per video, stated plainly ("I'd skip this if your skin is dry").
- Disclose AI voice in the description ("Voice: AI narration").

## Information integrity
- Every fact in captions/VO must come from products.json (researched) or from what is visible in the footage. If unverified, phrase as
  "reportedly" or drop it. Prices in KRW with USD conversion. No medical claims (treats acne, cures eczema). Use "may help with".
- Prefer facts foreigners cannot easily get: Korean retail price, Olive Young rank, launch date in Korea, Korean reviews gist.

## Sound
- Keep the natural footage sound under the VO (-14 dB) for texture shots. Music bed low (-20 dB), ducked under VO. Whoosh on scene
  changes, cash SFX on price reveals, pop on card reveals. Loudness target -14 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dBTP.

## Metadata
- TikTok: caption ≤150 chars + 4-6 hashtags (#kbeauty #koreanskincare #oliveyoung + product/ingredient tags). Ask a question in the caption.
- Reels: first line is the hook, 5-8 hashtags, mention "save this".
- Shorts: title ≤ 60 chars with the hook; description: facts list, prices, "Voice: AI narration", #Shorts #kbeauty.
- Cover text: 3-5 words, the hook noun, readable at thumbnail size.
